International Earth Science Olympiad

The International Earth Science Olympiad (IESO) is an annual competition for secondary school students from across the globe. India has participated in the IESO since 2007. The 19th edition of the IESO held in Turin, Italy over 20-27 August 2026 featured the Individual (Theory & Practical), Earth System Project (ESP), and International Team Field Investigation (ITFI) competitions.

I served as an invited faculty for the IESO pre-departure training camp held at Anna University, Chennai over 13-20 August 2026. I gave lectures on an interdisciplinary approach to understanding Earth’s mass extinctions, especially the deadliest one (the Permian Triassic mass extinction that took place ~ 252 million years ago) and their critical lessons for current global warming and climate change.
